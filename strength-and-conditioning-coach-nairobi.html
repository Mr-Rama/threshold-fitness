What strength, conditioning & endurance coaching covers

Strength training challenges muscles to produce force against resistance. It develops the ability to lift, push, pull or hold against an external load or the body’s own resistance.

Conditioning focuses on sustaining repeated efforts and recovering between them. It describes the capacity being trained, rather than one specific exercise format.

Endurance training develops the ability to continue physical activity over time, through sustained effort or repeated bouts depending on the activity.

These areas overlap but differ in emphasis: strength focuses on force, conditioning on repeated work and recovery, and endurance on continuing effort. A programme can combine them in different proportions depending on the activity.

They may be trained together, but each describes a different physical capacity and can involve different kinds of activity.

Frequently asked questions

Is strength and conditioning a separate booking?

No. Strength and conditioning coaching is delivered within one-to-one, in-person personal training.

Can I start training if I have no experience?

Absolutely. Just starting, or years in, your plan starts from where you are.

When is training available?

Training availability is Monday to Friday, 5:30am – 7:30pm, and Saturday, 7am – 12 noon. Sunday sessions or assessments may be arranged, subject to availability. Message us on WhatsApp to check current times.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule a session?

Let us know by 8pm the night before and we'll move your session to an available slot at no charge. Rescheduling depends on open slots in the schedule. Later cancellations, or cancelling once your coach is on the way, are charged as a full session. If we ever need to postpone because of an emergency, we'll reschedule at a time that suits you.

Can I train if I have an injury or health condition?

Tell us about any injury or health condition before starting. We may ask you to get medical clearance. Threshold Fitness provides fitness coaching, not medical diagnosis or treatment.
